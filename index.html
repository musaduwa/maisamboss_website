What we do

End-to-end supply, from farm gate to your facility

Two service lines, one standard of reliability, whether you need a steady stream of commodities or a contract delivered to specification.

Sourcing, Aggregation, Trading & Supply

We buy and aggregate ginger, onions, maize, soya beans, sesame, hibiscus flower, poultry and agro-allied products from trusted producers, then store, grade and distribute them to your specification.

  • Direct aggregation from farmer clusters and cooperatives
  • Cleaning, sorting, grading and bagging to buyer specs
  • Secure warehousing to protect quality and smooth out seasonality
  • Distribution nationwide and to ports for export

Procurement & Contract Supply

We source and supply agro-commodities, general goods and agricultural inputs such as seeds, fertilizer and equipment for private and public-sector contract execution.

  • Agricultural inputs: certified seeds, fertilizer, agro-chemicals and equipment
  • Government, NGO and development-programme contract delivery
  • General goods procurement with documented chain of custody
  • Delivery to schedule, with full documentation and reporting

How it works

A simple, transparent supply process

From your first enquiry to delivery, you know where your order is and what quality to expect.

  1. Step 1

    Enquiry

    Tell us the commodity, grade, volume and delivery location. We respond with availability and a quote.

  2. Step 2

    Sourcing & Quality Check

    We aggregate from vetted farmers, then clean, sort and test each lot for moisture, purity and grade.

  3. Step 3

    Storage & Logistics

    Goods are bagged and held in secure storage while we arrange transport, documentation and scheduling.

  4. Step 4

    Delivery

    On-time delivery to your facility, warehouse or port, with weighbridge tickets and quality records.

Why choose us

Built for buyers who can’t afford supply surprises

  • Direct farmer network

    We buy straight from farmer clusters and cooperatives, which gives us consistent volumes, traceability and fair prices.

  • Quality assurance & grading

    Every lot is cleaned, sorted and checked for moisture, purity and foreign matter before dispatch.

  • Secure storage facilities

    Clean, ventilated and pest-controlled warehousing keeps your commodities in top condition.

  • Nationwide distribution

    Our logistics partners deliver across Nigeria and to major ports on schedule.

  • Export documentation support

    Help with phytosanitary certificates, certificates of origin, NEPC requirements and inspection.

  • Transparent pricing

    Clear, itemised quotes with no hidden charges, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
